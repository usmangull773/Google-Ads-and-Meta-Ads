Paid Acquisition Snapshot

Getting Midas sign-ups and demos ready to advertise

A quick outside-in read of what is in place today, what is missing before paid can work, and how a first 30-day test would run.
Based only on what is publicly observable from the outside

What is already in place

Web analytics on the site

GA4 is live on the marketing site, so there is a real analytics foundation to build on.

Product analytics in the app

The app runs PostHog, so in-product behaviour is being captured. A good starting point.

What is missing before paid can work

The site and the app do not join up
GA4 stops at the marketing site. The sign-up app (app.getmidas.ai) has product analytics but no GA4 and no ad tracking, so a completed sign-up never reaches Google or Meta.
Demo bookings are not measurable
The demo button sends people to an off-site Google Calendar link, so a booked demo is not captured as a conversion today.
No conversion signal or audience for ads
There is no Google Ads tag and no Meta pixel on the site, and GA4 sits behind cookie consent. So there is nothing yet to optimise toward and no retargeting audience for either platform.
Nothing is running yet, which is an advantage
No live Google ads appear in Google's public Ads Transparency Center, and no Meta ads presence was found. A clean start means no wasted spend to unwind, and no bad data to clean up.
Market context: the category is already being bid on by incumbents such as FactSet and Bloomberg. That is exactly why a precise, tracking-first entry matters: it keeps cost per sign-up and per demo honest from day one instead of burning budget to learn what could have been measured up front.

Why this is the real issue

Until a sign-up and a demo can be measured end to end, no paid channel can be judged or scaled with confidence. The tracking has to come before the spend, not after. That is the core gap here, and it is quick to close.

How it gets fixed

01
One measurement layer across the site and the app, so a sign-up and a demo are both counted, wherever they happen.
02
Google and Meta conversion tracking built correctly, server-side where it counts, so the numbers hold up.
03
Audiences switched on: site visitors, sign-ups, look-alikes, and interest, ready for retargeting and prospecting.
